# M&M Munchies FAQ

## How do I place an order?
Pick a restaurant, add dishes to your cart, open the cart, choose a payment method on the payment page and place the order. You will see your bill after the order is placed.

## Where can I see my past orders?
Open "My Orders" after logging in. Each order opens as a bill with the items, taxes and total.

## Is there a minimum order value?
There is no minimum order value. Orders below ₹500 pay a ₹40 delivery fee, and orders of ₹500 or more get free delivery.

## Can I order only vegetarian food?
Yes. Every dish is marked as vegetarian or non-vegetarian, and you can use the veg-only filter at the top of the app.

## How spicy is a dish?
Dishes have a spice level from 0 (mild) to 3 (very spicy). Spicier dishes show chilli icons on the menu.

## Can I change my order after placing it?
Orders cannot be edited. You can cancel within 5 minutes and place a new one.

## What if my food is wrong or missing?
Contact support with your order number from the bill. Missing or wrong items are refunded to the original payment method.

## Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?
Menus do not have a vegan or gluten-free label. You can check the allergen list on each dish, which shows gluten and dairy.
