# M&M Munchies Policies

## Ordering and login
- You must be logged in to place an order.
- You can browse restaurants and menus without logging in.
- You can order from more than one restaurant in the same cart.

## Payment methods
We accept three payment methods: UPI, Credit/Debit Card, and Cash on Delivery.

## Delivery fee
- Orders with a food subtotal below ₹500 have a delivery fee of ₹40.
- Orders with a food subtotal of ₹500 or more have free delivery.

## Taxes
A 5% GST is added to the food subtotal on every order.

## Delivery time
Each restaurant shows an estimated delivery time in minutes on its card. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## Delivery areas
M&M Munchies is available in Mumbai, Pune, Delhi and Bangalore.

## Cancellation and refunds
- An order can be cancelled within 5 minutes of placing it.
- Once the restaurant has started preparing the order, it cannot be cancelled.
- If an order is cancelled in time, or an item is missing or wrong, the amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
- Cash on Delivery orders have nothing to refund if they are cancelled before delivery.

## Allergens
Each dish lists the common allergens it contains (such as gluten, dairy, nuts, egg, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame). Kitchens handle many ingredients, so traces of other allergens may be present. If you have a serious allergy, please contact the restaurant before ordering.
